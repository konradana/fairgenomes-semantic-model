SNVs	A variation of a single nucleotide at a specific location of the genome due to base substitution, which is found at any frequency in the population.	NCIT	C164674	http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/NCIT_C164674
Indels	A mutation class that includes insertion mutations, deletion mutations and mutation events where both an insertion and a deletion has occurred.	NCIT	C99752	http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/NCIT_C99752
SVs	Any mutation with an origin in cells that are not destined to become gametes. As a consequence, such mutations are not transmitted to progeny, though they will be transmitted during any mitosis within the individual. Somatic mutations may contribute to a broad variety of pathologies including cancer.	NCIT	C18060	http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/NCIT_C18060
CNVs	Variation in the number of copies of a particular sequence within the genetic material of an individual. Large-scale copy number polymorphisms are common and widely distributed in the human genome.	NCIT	C40207	http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/NCIT_C40207
Gene Fusions	Any hybrid gene formed from two previously separate genes. Such fusions occur as a result of translocation, intersititial deletion or chromosomal inversion, and often result in gene products with functions different from the two fusion partners. Gene fusions are associated frequently with hematological cancers, sarcomas and prostate cancer.	NCIT	C20195	http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/NCIT_C20195
